# Analysis of a Euchre hand

### Purpose

  The purpose of this project is to learn more about data science / machine learning, Pythonskills, problem solving, and the probabilities that lie within Euchre.

### Representations + The data structure

  We are representing cards as a tuple with two items. The first is the suit, listed below. The second is the strength of the card, also listed below.

#### Suits

**DIAMONDS** - 1
**HEARTS**   - 2
**CLUBS**    - 3
**SPADES**   - 4

#### Powers

9 = 9
10 = 10
Jack = 11 (Not bower)
Queen = 12
King = 13
Ace = 14
Left bower = 15
Right bower = 16
